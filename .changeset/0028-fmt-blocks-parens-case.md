`papyrus fmt` sets functions, events, states, structs, groups and full properties apart with a blank line, drops the parentheses around a whole `If`, `ElseIf` or `While` condition, and writes keywords in PascalCase (`EndFunction`) with `keywords = "pascal"` in the `[fmt]` section of `papyrus.toml`.
